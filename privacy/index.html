Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This policy explains what happens to the information you send to FMUSER through this website. It covers business enquiries about digital TV headend equipment — there is no customer account, subscription or payment on this site.

1. What we collect

  • Enquiry details you type in: your name, email address, company (if you give it), the subject you select and the message itself, including any model numbers or project details you include.
  • Technical data from your browser request: like any web server, the hosting provider of this site may keep standard access records — IP address, browser type, pages requested and time — for security and troubleshooting.
  • What we do not collect: we do not ask for payment details on this site, and there is no account to register.

2. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a quotation for the models you ask about.
  • To keep a record of the technical details agreed for an order, so support and warranty questions can be handled later.
  • To protect the site against spam and abusive form submissions.

3. Third-party services used by this site

Being specific about this is easier than a paragraph of generalities:

  • Form delivery — Web3Forms. The contact and quote forms are submitted toapi.web3forms.com, which relays the message to our mailbox. The content of your message passes through that service.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) supplies the typeface, andcdnjs / Cloudflare supplies the icon set used in the interface. Your browser requests those files directly, so those providers see your IP address as part of serving them.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN serves the styling framework used to render the pages.

4. Cookies and analytics

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies on this site, and no analytics or advertising tag is installed here. If that changes, this page will say which service is used and what it collects.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiry correspondence is kept while it is relevant to the business relationship — for example while a quotation is open, or for as long as the order and warranty records need to be available. Ask us to delete your enquiry and we will remove it, unless we are required to keep a record for accounting or legal reasons.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. It is shared only where it is needed to answer you: the form delivery service described above, our own mail provider, and — for a specific order — the freight forwarder or customs broker handling that shipment.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are encrypted in transit. Access to enquiry mail is limited to the people who prepare quotations and support the orders.

8. Your requests

Write to sales@cableiptvheadend.com to ask what we hold about your enquiry, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Tell us the email address you used when you wrote to us so we can find the record.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way this site handles information changes — for example if we add an analytics service — this page is updated and the date at the top changes.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: sales@cableiptvheadend.com. Technical or sales enquiries: use the contact form.
