The FMUSER FUQ-4655L Encoder Modulator integrates encoding, multiplexing, scrambling, and modulating functions. It processes signals from 12 HDMI inputs and IP inputs, encoding to MPEG2 or H.264, and outputs via RF for DVB-C, DVB-T/ATSC, or ISDB-T standards. Managed through web-based interface for control and updates. Ideal for small CATV headend systems, broadcasting, and IP streaming applications. Key feature Supports 12 HDMI inputs with MPEG2 & H.264 encoding, Features DVB-C, DVB-T/ATSC, and ISDB-T modulation for RF output, Integrated multiplexing and scrambling with PID remapping, Provides IP I/O over UDP/RTP/RTSP protocols, Web-based management for easy control
