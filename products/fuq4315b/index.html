The FUQ-4315E is a 4-channel encoder with CVBS/S-Video/YPbPr inputs, supporting MPEG-2 video and MPEG-1 Layer II audio encoding. It processes signals through ASI input multiplexing to generate MPTS streams, with outputs via IP and ASI. This unit features LCD+keyboard control and Web NMS/SNMP management. Ideal for digital headends in cable and satellite broadcasting systems.
