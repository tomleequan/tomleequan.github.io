The FMUSER FUQ-5315K is a multi-channel encoder with 4/8/12 HDMI inputs, supporting MPEG-4 AVC/H.264 video and LC-AAC/HE-AAC audio encoding. It streams content via UDP, RTSP, RTMP, HTTP, and HLS protocols for reception on PCs and mobile devices. Controlled through web-based management, it enables easy updates. Ideal for professional IP broadcasting in live streaming and digital media distribution.
