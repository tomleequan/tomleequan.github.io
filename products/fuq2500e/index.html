The FMUSER FUQ-2500E Agile Modulator offers RF modulation with 1 audio (L/R) and 1 video (CVBS) input, plus configurable RF output. It processes signals using video input AGC for stability. This unit features a 19-inch 1U rack-mount design and keyboard/LCD operation with online upgrade. Ideal for hotels, communities, and entertainment venues.
