FUQ4417J ISDB-T Modulator 24-48 Channels IP to RF

  • ModelFUQ4417J

The FMUSER FUQ-4417J is a high-density ISDB-T modulator converting IP streams to 24/48 RF carriers (50-960MHz) through 4xRJ45 and 2xSFP ports. It processes transport streams with PID remapping and PCR correction, featuring web-based control and 1U rack-mount design. Ideal for digital TV headends, cable network upgrades, and multiscreen distribution systems requiring simultaneous RF/IP output.
