The FMUSER FUQ-200E is a 10G IP QAM processor integrating multiplexer, scrambler, and modulator functions. It processes signals through multiplexing, scrambling, and modulation, supporting up to 112 DVB-C QAM channels with 3 SFP+(10G)/SFP(1G) ports and 2 GE ports. Housed in a 1U chassis with modular card slots, it features web-based network management and is ideal for cable television headends and broadcast systems requiring high-density processing.This processor is equipped with 2 card slots to load 32ch or 48ch or 64ch QAM card. Its high density helps operators to save cost to the most extent.
