The FMUSER FUQ-4419R (V2) is a high-density DVB-T modulator converting IP inputs to RF outputs. It processes signals via modulation with inputs including max 1024 IP streams through 4 GE/SFP ports and 6 ASI inputs. Controlled through web-based management for seamless operation. Ideal for next-generation DTV broadcasting systems and cable headends.
