The FMUSER FUQ-4653D is an encoder modulator with MPEG2 HD and MPEG4 HD encoding, featuring inputs: 2x HDMI, YPbPr, CVBS, and outputs: RF, 2x ASI, IP. It converts signals through encoding and modulation for digital broadcasting. Controlled via web-based NMS, it supports remote management and updates. Ideal for hotels, metros, and public venue applications.
