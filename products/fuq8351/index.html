The FUQ-8351 is a professional indoor digital transmitter supporting DVB-T/ATSC/ISDB-T standards with 10-100W adjustable RF output (470-806MHz). It processes signals via 4/16/32/64 QAM modulation and features AGC-controlled power stability (±0.25dB). The 2U rack-mount unit includes LED alarms for real-time fault monitoring, designed for SFN/MFN networks in broadcast studios and mobile TV systems.
