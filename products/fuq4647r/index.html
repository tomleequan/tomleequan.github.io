The FMUSER FUQ-4647B is an encoder modulator integrating encoding, multiplexing, and modulation in a 1RU chassis. It processes inputs including 8/16/24 HDMI, 1 ASI, 1 USB player, and 128 IP streams, encoding with MPEG-4 AVC/H.264 and outputting 12 DVB-C RF carriers plus ASI/IP mirrors. Featuring web-based control for remote management, it is ideal for compact CATV headends and hospitality TV systems.
