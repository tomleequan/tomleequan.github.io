FUQ4825B IP Decoder 8 IP Inputs to 4 HDMI Outputs

The FMUSER FUQ-4825B decodes 8 IP inputs via UDP/RTP, supporting MPEG-2/H.264/H.265/AVS/AVS+ formats. It outputs signals to 4 HDMI channels with embedded stereo audio, enabling manual resolution scaling up to 1080p. Controlled through web-based interface, this decoder is ideal for flexible program distribution in broadcasting and digital signage applications.
