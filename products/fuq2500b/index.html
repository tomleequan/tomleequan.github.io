FUQ2500B Analog RF Modulator 32-64 Channel

  • ModelFUQ2500B
  • CategoryModulator / Modem
  • DatasheetPDF

The FMUSER FUQ-2500B converts IP/HDMI/AV inputs to analog RF outputs for up to 64 channels. It processes signals with full digital processing for high SFDR performance, supporting A2 and NICAM728 audio standards and PAL/NTSC/SECAM modulation including OSD insertion. Applications include television broadcasting for PAL, NTSC, or SECAM systems.
