The FUQ-4339R Encoder is a professional HD/SD audio and video encoding device with scalable 8/16/24 HDMI inputs and outputs including 1 MPTS and 8 SPTS per module. It processes signals using MPEG4 AVC/H.264 video encoding and MPEG1 Layer II, LC-AAC, HE-AAC audio formats. Controlled via web management, it supports features like QR code and caption insertion. Ideal for cable TV head-ends and broadcasting systems.
