The FMUSER FUQ-3902B is a professional DVB-S/S2/S2X demodulator, descrambler, and IP multiplexer with 2 tuner inputs (950-2150MHz) and 32 IP stream outputs (UDP/RTP). It demodulates satellite signals, decrypts via CAM/CI or BISS, and remuxes up to 32 channels into a single MPTS output. This 1RU rack-mount unit supports DiSEqC control and web-based NMS, ideal for broadcast headends and IPTV distribution.
