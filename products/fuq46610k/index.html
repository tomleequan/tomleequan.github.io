The FMUSER FUQ-46610K processes 6 channels of analog/digital signals via 470-860MHz RF input and delivers 48-960MHz RF output. It converts between DVB-C/T, ATSC, and ISDB-T standards with configurable 6M/8M bandwidth. This 1RU rack-mount unit features WEB-based management for remote control and firmware updates. Ideal for cable headend modernization and multi-standard broadcast systems requiring flexible signal conversion.
