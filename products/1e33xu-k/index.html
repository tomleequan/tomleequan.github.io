The FMUSER IPTV HD STB is an Android 7.1-based set-top box with 4K H.265/HEVC decoding and HDMI 2.0 output. It processes IPTV streams via 10/100M Ethernet or dual-band Wi-Fi, supporting Widevine L1 DRM and custom EPG interfaces. The compact device integrates ad systems and runs commercial apps, ideal for hospitality TV systems and community IPTV deployments.
