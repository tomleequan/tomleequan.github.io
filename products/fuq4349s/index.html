The FMUSER FUQ-4349S is an 8-in-1 FM receiver that captures FM audio and outputs via ASI and IP ports. It compresses audio into digital TS streams using MPEG1 Layer 2 encoding. Features LCD/keyboard control and web-based NMS for management. Ideal for professional audio distribution in HFC or terrestrial systems, integrating with STBs or TVs.Each antenna receives two audios and this unit can maximally process 8 audios. In addition, it is equipped with one ASI input port as well for Audio/Video re-mux. It is featured to operators with its high capacity and integration.
